Architecture Flow

1. Source Systems Layer: Core banking, ERP, treasury, CRM, trading systems, and regulatory feeds provide structured and semi-structured financial data.

2. Data Ingestion & Integration: API-based ingestion, streaming connectors, and scheduled batch pipelines normalize financial events into governed schemas.

3. Semantic Intelligence Layer: AI-governed KPI definitions with lineage tracking ensure NIM, ROA, ROE, cost-to-income, and risk metrics are consistently defined across the enterprise.

4. Analytics & Visualization Layer: Executive dashboards, regulatory templates, and board-ready financial scorecards with drill-down audit traceability.

5. Governance & Security: Role-based access control, row-level security, metadata lineage, and compliance logging.

Use Case – FinTech Risk & Performance Monitoring

A digital bank operating across multiple jurisdictions requires daily liquidity risk, capital adequacy, and profitability dashboards.

  • Transactions stream from core banking into the normalized model.
  • Liquidity ratios and capital buffers are computed in near real-time.
  • Board dashboards show stress-test simulations.
  • Regulatory reports auto-generate using pre-aligned compliance schemas.

Outcome: Reporting cycle reduced from 5 days to same-day visibility with full audit lineage.

Technical Pipeline Design

  • Hybrid ingestion (batch + real-time event streaming)
  • Schema normalization with financial domain models
  • Data quality enforcement & reconciliation checkpoints
  • Metric computation engine (calculated KPIs, ratios, trend analytics)
  • Semantic layer abstraction for governed AI querying
  • API layer for downstream integrations
