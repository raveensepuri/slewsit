FinTech & Capital Markets

Secure, low-latency, and regulator-aligned technology platforms for financial institutions, digital banks, and capital markets firms.

Core Industry Challenges

  • Real-time risk aggregation across fragmented systems
  • Regulatory reporting (Basel III, IFRS, SOX)
  • Low-latency trading and settlement platforms
  • Data governance and audit traceability
  • Elimination of spreadsheet-based workflows

Use Case Scenarios

1. Real-Time Risk & Liquidity Monitoring

Integrate trading, treasury, and core banking data into governed dashboards with live exposure metrics and automated risk thresholds.

2. Regulatory Reporting Automation

Automate generation of capital adequacy, liquidity coverage ratio (LCR), and stress test reporting with built-in validation logic.

3. Digital Bank KPI Intelligence

Provide executives with real-time visibility into NIM, ROA, cost-to-income, and portfolio performance across customer segments.

4. Secure Cloud-Native Trading Infrastructure

Deploy hardened Kubernetes clusters for algorithmic trading engines with full RBAC, audit logging, and network isolation.
